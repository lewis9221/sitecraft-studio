Paint mountains, valleys, coastlines and lakes directly onto the ground. Drag on the 3D world to sculpt.

✨ Quick start
1. Pick Raise or Lower below (or use the floating brush bar).
2. Drag your mouse across the land. You'll see a bright circular brush preview.
3. Use Size for a wider brush and Strength for faster changes.
4. Right-drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, or use the Top view button.
5. Ctrl+Z undoes your last stroke. Keys 1–8 select brushes.
